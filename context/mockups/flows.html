Planned Features
📊
AUM Flow per Category
Daily net inflow/outflow (AUM delta) for each ETF, sourced from public ETF data providers. Shows where institutional money is actually going, independent of price action.
📈
Flow Momentum
Rolling 20-day cumulative net flows vs 60-day average — identifies when capital is accelerating into or out of a sector. A strong positive flow momentum alongside RS signal = conviction confirmation.
⚡
Buy / Sell Pressure
Normalized buy/sell indicator combining flow direction, volume relative to 30-day average, and price confirmation. Displayed as a color-coded bar per category.
🔗
Flow + Signal Composite
When AUM flows align with the existing RS and macro signals, the composite score will gain an additional flow-confirmation weight. Reduces false positives from price-only signals.
📅
Flow History Timeline
12-month rolling chart of net AUM flows per category, with regime overlay. See how institutional allocation shifted across Risk On / Risk Off cycles.
🔔
Flow-Based Alert Rules
New alert conditions: "net_inflow_7d crosses +$500M", "flow_momentum diverges from RS_60", or "consecutive outflow days > 5". Available in the Alerts page once flows are live.
Planned Data Sources
· ETF.com or VettaFi public AUM data (scraped or API) · SSGA (State Street), Vanguard, iShares fund flow disclosures · Ingestion pipeline extension: new FLOWS ingestion job alongside existing PRICES + MACRO jobs · Expected frequency: daily, with 1–2 day reporting lag from providers
